0What I held fixed, and what I changed

Inherited, not approximated

  • 1024 plate, rx 230, vertical #FFD08A → #F2895C, two stops, never rotated
  • Both landscape colours verbatim: #B0532F at 90% behind, #7A2F1C in front
  • Landscape bleeds off left, right and bottom — a scene, not a centred glyph, so the mark maps 1:1
  • Subject seated so it reads at 16px as one mass on water

Armada's own

  • The ink: #FFF6E8 at 92% — between cupertino's #FFF8EC and bastion's #FDEFDC, its own value
  • The landscape: two periodic waves instead of two hills. Periods 512 and 680 units, amplitudes 34 and 44, so they never beat in phase — against cupertino's 180-unit ridge and bastion's 137, water is a texture where hills are a mass
  • The seat: the near wave crosses the subject. Cupertino sets behind, Bastion stands on, Armada floats in — three constructions from one grammar
