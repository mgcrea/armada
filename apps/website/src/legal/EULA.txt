8. UPDATES

Updates within 1.x are free and are published as new downloads.

The app can also check for them itself. That check is off until you turn it on
or press Check Now, and you may leave it off and download new versions by hand.
When it runs, it reads one file, armada.mgcrea.io/appcast.xml, which redirects to
the latest GitHub release, and it sends no identifier with the request: not your
licence key, not a machine id. Your IP address is visible to the servers that
answer, as it is for any download. Every update is signed, and the signature is
verified before anything is installed.

9. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES WITH YOUR DATA

Armada reads the session files Claude Code and Codex already write to your Mac,
in ~/.claude* and ~/.codex, and opens them read only. It never writes to either
tool's configuration, holds no vendor credentials, and never opens Codex's
auth.json.

What it writes is its own: its preferences, usage history in
~/Library/Application Support/io.mgcrea.armada (kept 30 days, and deletable from
Settings), and a startup script in its own temporary folder when you start a
session.

Two honest limits on that:

  (a) To read plan limits, Armada runs your own installed claude and asks it a
      question. That program talks to Anthropic over your own sign-in, under
      Anthropic's terms, as it does whenever you use it.

  (b) A session Armada starts or forks for you runs claude or codex in your
      terminal. What that agent then does is between you and its vendor.

Armada is not sandboxed. That is by design: it has to read other tools' folders.

10. ACCESSIBILITY AND INPUT

Accessibility permission, which you grant and may withdraw at any time in System
Settings. A mouse binding sees only the middle and extra mouse buttons: left and
right clicks, movement, scrolling and keystrokes never reach it.
